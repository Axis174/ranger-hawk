High Uintas Central

Mountain Goat

Duchesne and Summit counties—Boundary begins where the Lake Fork River crosses the USFS boundary; north along the Lake Fork River to Oweep Creek; north along this creek to USFS Trail 042; north on this trail to Squaw Pass and USFS Trail 103; north on this trail to the East Fork of the Blacks Fork River; north along this river to the Blacks Fork river; north along this river to Meeks Cabin Reservoir and the Utah-Wyoming state line; east along this state line to the Henrys Fork River; south along this river to USFS Trail 117; south on this trail to Gunsight Pass and USFS Trail 068; east on this trail through Painter Basin to USFS Trail 044; east on this trail to the Uinta River; south along this river to the USFS boundary; west along this boundary to the Lake Fork River. USGS 1:100,000 Maps: Kings Peak. Boundary questions? Call the Vernal office, 435-781-9459, or the Ogden office, 801-476-2740. - A hunting permit does not authorize the permit holder to hunt on Native American trust lands, CWMUs (unless you specifically have a permit for that CWMU) or on National Park lands. Furthermore, it is the responsibility of hunters to learn if hunting is allowed and what specific rules and regulations may apply on National Monuments, National Wildlife Refuges, State Parks, UDWR Wildlife and Waterfowl Management areas, military installations and within the boundaries of cities, towns and municipalities. Written permission is required to hunt private lands.
